— FACILITIES

SMART LABS

Twelve specialized laboratories with pilot-scale processing equipment.

PILOT PLANT ⚙️

Food Processing Pilot Plant

Pasteurizers, spray dryer, extruder and retort systems — run real production batches at pilot scale.

ISO 17025 🦠

Food Microbiology Lab

Pathogen detection, probiotic culturing and shelf-life studies in a BSL-2 certified facility.

GC-MS · HPLC 📊

Analytical & Instrumentation

Quantify nutrients, toxins and adulterants with GC-MS, HPLC, FTIR and atomic absorption spectrometry.

AI + CV 🤖

Sensory & Digital Evaluation

Trained taste panels plus computer-vision texture analysis and electronic tongue/nose systems.

SUSTAINABLE 📦

Packaging Innovation Lab

Biodegradable films, active packaging and shelf-life testing chambers for next-gen materials.

STARTUP 🚀

Product Development Studio

Concept-to-shelf studio where student teams formulation-test and consumer-test their own products.
